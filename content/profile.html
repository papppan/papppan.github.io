I am a member of the Theoretical Physics faculty at The Institute of Mathematical Sciences, Chennai. My research interests are in particle physics with an emphasis on the physics of hadrons, a family of particles/excitations the protons and neutrons are part of. I perform numerical simulations of the underlying fundamental theory, Quantum ChromoDynamics (QCD), to predict several yet-to-be-discovered particles and their properties or interpret the nature and origin of the discovered ones at various particle collider-detector facilities such as those at LHCb and Belle. Often, these simulations are computation-intensive calculations performed at leading High-Performance Computing (HPC) facilities inside India and abroad. Some of my topical research interests are

  • Conventional hadrons (mesons and baryons)
  • Multi-hadron scattering in a finite volume
  • Hadronic molecules, tetraquarks, pentaquarks, hexaquarks, etc.
  • Charm and bottom hadron excitations
  • Exotic hadrons, XYZTP
  • Hadrons at extreme conditions
